Contacts file (vCard)

Exported from your phone, Google Contacts, Apple Contacts or Outlook (.vcf). People come in as acquaintances unless you tick the box.

Calendar (iCalendar)

An .ics export. Past meetings are logged as interactions, future ones as plans. Events with only you are skipped; meetings with more than 8 people don't count as being in touch. Add your own email to your profile so you aren't listed as an attendee.

Spreadsheet (CSV)

Any CSV with a header row, including LinkedIn's Connections export and Google Contacts CSV. Columns are matched automatically; you can adjust the matching on the preview.

Restore from a JSON export

Restores into a brand-new vault from the command line: ties --vault new.vault restore export.json.
